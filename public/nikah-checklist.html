THE SHIA NIKAH CHECKLIST  ·  A STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE FOR FAMILIES

A nikah is too important to leave to memory and momentum. Work through every stage together as a family — tick each box with honesty — so nothing that matters gets missed in the excitement.

Stage 1 — Before the search

  • Write down what your family is looking for: marja followed, practice expectations, education & career direction, location flexibility, timeline
  • Decide who is involved and how: who acts as wali/guardian, how families will communicate, what privacy to expect
  • Agree as a family on non-negotiables vs. preferences — before any profile is browsed

Stage 2 — Vetting a prospect

  • Check consistency: does their description of daily practice match how they actually live?
  • Is their family involved early and openly? Note direct vs. evasive answers
  • Verify the practical: identity, education/employment claims, marital history
  • Through trusted mutual contacts, ask about character and reputation

Stage 3 — The families meet

  • Exchange family backgrounds honestly — no surprises later
  • Discuss living arrangements, finances, and religious practice in the household
  • Let both sides ask anything — the awkward questions now prevent regret later
  • If at a distance: use video, keep the wali in the loop at every step

Stage 4 — The nikah contract

  • Agree the mahr clearly — amount, form, and timing — before the ceremony
  • Put any additional lawful conditions in writing as part of the contract
  • Confirm particulars (witnesses, recitation, the wali's role) with your marja/scholar — rulings vary
  • Arrange the nikah nama documentation your locality requires; keep copies with both families

Stage 5 — After the nikah

  • Plan the transition: housing, finances, and the household's religious life together
  • Keep communication open between families in the early months
  • Remember: most new-marriage friction is logistical, not fundamental — early support prevents hardening
Note: This is a practical family organizer, not a fiqh manual. Confirm all religious particulars with the marja your family follows.
