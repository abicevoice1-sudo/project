The idea. Two wedding bands weave over–under into an S — two lives, one bond, the initial of ShiaRishta. Where they cross, an 8-pointed Rub el Hizb star (۞) marks the nikah: the moment two families join. The motif comes from classical Islamic geometry; this rendering is drawn from scratch on our own grid, which is what makes the expression original.

The colors. Every tone is pulled from the site's own palette — deep sage, warm paper, burnished gold — so the mark sits naturally in the header instead of fighting it. The old neon emerald is gone.

On originality. No stock icon, no template, no traced artwork: every coordinate above was authored for this mark. That originality is the real protection against copy claims. A trademark attorney's clearance search is still the right step before you register the mark — say the word and I'll prep the usage sheet they'll ask for.
